Deinopidae · Net-casting spider

Ogre-faced spider

It doesn’t wait in a web. It holds a net of silk in its legs and throws it over prey.

01 — The face

Built for the dark.

Its light-sensitive eyes give it some of the best nocturnal vision in the world.

02 — The net

A net made of legs.

It attaches its silk to its legs, then spreads them far apart to form a net. When it feels prey nearby, it catches it.

03 — Light

Sensitive, and fragile.

The eyes are photosensitive and sunlight burns them, so the spider has to regrow its eyes. Change the light and watch the eyes.

04 — Anatomy

Slim by design.

A small head, a long tapered abdomen and eight jointed legs, hanging from a silk line. Switch on X-ray to see the wireframe.

05 — The ledge

Leave the dark.

Back along the cliff, away from the thread, the net and those eyes.

06 — The mouth

Daylight.

The cave opens onto a ridge. The spider never has to come out, because its prey comes to it.

07 — Outside

A quiet valley.

In real life this spider is only a few centimetres long, and by day it looks like a twig.
